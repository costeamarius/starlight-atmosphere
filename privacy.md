Privacy Policy – Starlight Atmosphere
Last updated: November 3, 2026

Starlight Atmosphere is developed by Marius Costea ("Starlight Atmos").

Data collection: Starlight Atmosphere does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal information. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no telemetry, and the overlay does not connect to the internet.

Local settings: Your preferences (selected theme, language, display level) are saved only on your own computer and never leave it. Uninstalling the app removes them.

External links: The app contains an optional "Support the developer" link that opens Buy Me a Coffee in your web browser. Any information you provide there is handled by Buy Me a Coffee under its own privacy policy. Donations are voluntary and unlock nothing in the app.

Children: The app does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

Local settings: Your preferences (selected theme, language, display level) and a small diagnostic log are saved only on your own computer and never leave it. Uninstalling the Microsoft Store version removes them.

Changes: Any update to this policy will be published at this address with a new date.

Contact: For questions, contact the developer through the Microsoft Store listing or https://buymeacoffee.com/mariuscostea
